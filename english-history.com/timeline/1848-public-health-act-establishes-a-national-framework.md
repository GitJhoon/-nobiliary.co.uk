# Public Health Act establishes a national framework (1848)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1848-public-health-act-establishes-a-national-framework
Categories: main-event

Parliament created a General Board of Health and enabled local sanitary reforms. Within the broader Victorian Britain period, industrial society, mass politics, public-health reform, global empire and the Irish question dominated public life. This event is placed at 1848 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The act was an important step toward modern public-health administration.

## Why it matters
