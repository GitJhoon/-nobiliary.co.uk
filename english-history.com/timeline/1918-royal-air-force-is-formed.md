# Royal Air Force is formed (1918)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1918-royal-air-force-is-formed
Categories: main-event

The Royal Flying Corps and Royal Naval Air Service were merged into an independent air force. Within the broader Early 20th Century & World Wars period, mass democracy, the two world wars, Irish partition, economic crisis and the rise of Labour remade the political and social order. This event is placed at 1 April 1918 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The RAF became the world's first independent national air force.

## Why it matters
The RAF became the world's first independent national air force.

Ruler at the time: [George V](https://english-history.com/rulers/george-v)
