# Synod of Whitby (664)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/664-synod-of-whitby
Categories: main-event

King Oswiu chose Roman rather than Irish practices for the Northumbrian church. Within the broader Post-Roman Britain period, Roman institutions gave way to competing Brittonic and Anglo-Saxon kingdoms while Christianity was rebuilt through continental and Irish missions. This event is placed at AD 664 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The decision aligned much of the English church more closely with Roman ecclesiastical customs.
