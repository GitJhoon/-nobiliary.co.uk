# Public Health Act consolidates sanitary law (1875)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1875-public-health-act-consolidates-sanitary-law
Categories: main-event

The act required local authorities to address sewers, water, nuisances and disease prevention. Within the broader Victorian Britain period, industrial society, mass politics, public-health reform, global empire and the Irish question dominated public life. This event is placed at 1875 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. It helped institutionalize public health as a core responsibility of local government.

## Why it matters
It helped institutionalize public health as a core responsibility of local government.
