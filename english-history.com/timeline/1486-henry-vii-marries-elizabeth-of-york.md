# Henry VII marries Elizabeth of York (1486)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1486-henry-vii-marries-elizabeth-of-york
Categories: main-event

Henry married the Yorkist heiress Elizabeth, symbolically uniting the rival houses. Within the broader Tudor Britain period, dynastic consolidation, Reformation, closer incorporation of Wales and Ireland, and rivalry with Catholic Europe transformed the state. This event is placed at 18 January 1486 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The marriage strengthened Tudor legitimacy and became central to later dynastic propaganda.
