# Stamp Act crisis in the American colonies (1765)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1765-stamp-act-crisis-in-the-american-colonies
Categories: main-event

Parliament imposed a direct tax on printed materials in North America and met organized resistance. Within the broader Georgian Britain period, parliamentary government, industrialization, imperial expansion, war with France and political reform turned Britain into a global commercial and military power. This event is placed at 1765 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The dispute sharpened arguments about taxation, representation and imperial authority.
