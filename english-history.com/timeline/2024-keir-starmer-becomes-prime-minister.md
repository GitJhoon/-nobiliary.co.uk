# Keir Starmer becomes prime minister (2024)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/2024-keir-starmer-becomes-prime-minister
Categories: ruler-change

Starmer was appointed prime minister after Labour's general-election victory. Within the broader Postwar & Modern UK period, decolonization, welfare institutions, migration, European integration and withdrawal, devolution and social change reshaped the modern United Kingdom. This event is placed at 5 July 2024 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. His accession began the first Labour government since 2010.

## Why it matters
His accession began the first Labour government since 2010.
