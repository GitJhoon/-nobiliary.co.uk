# European Reformation ideas begin reaching Britain (1517)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1517-european-reformation-ideas-begin-reaching-britain

Luther's challenge to the papacy quickly circulated among scholars and merchants. Within the broader Tudor Britain period, dynastic consolidation, Reformation, closer incorporation of Wales and Ireland, and rivalry with Catholic Europe transformed the state. This event is placed at 1517 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The new religious arguments provided an intellectual context for later English and Scottish reformations.
