# Angevin Empire takes shape (1154)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1154-angevin-empire-takes-shape
Categories: main-event

Henry II ruled England together with extensive lands in western France through inheritance and marriage. Within the broader Norman & Angevin Britain period, Norman conquest, cross-Channel lordship and royal reform created a new aristocracy and a more centralized monarchy. This event is placed at 1154–1189 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The cross-Channel realm made English politics inseparable from French dynastic rivalry.
