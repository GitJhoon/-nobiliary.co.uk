# South Sea Bubble collapses (1720)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1720-south-sea-bubble-collapses
Categories: main-event

Speculation in South Sea Company shares ended in a spectacular financial crash. Within the broader Georgian Britain period, parliamentary government, industrialization, imperial expansion, war with France and political reform turned Britain into a global commercial and military power. This event is placed at 1720 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The scandal damaged confidence in government finance and became a lasting symbol of speculative mania.

## Why it matters
