# Boudica's revolt is defeated (61)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/61-boudica-s-revolt-is-defeated
Categories: battle

Roman forces under Suetonius Paulinus defeated the rebel army in a decisive battle. Within the broader Roman Britain period, conquest, provincial administration, urbanization, frontier warfare and the gradual weakening of imperial rule reshaped the island. This event is placed at AD 61 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The victory secured Roman rule in southern Britain for centuries.
