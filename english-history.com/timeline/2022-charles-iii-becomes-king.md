# Charles III becomes king (2022)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/2022-charles-iii-becomes-king
Categories: ruler-change

Charles automatically succeeded his mother as monarch. Within the broader Postwar & Modern UK period, decolonization, welfare institutions, migration, European integration and withdrawal, devolution and social change reshaped the modern United Kingdom. This event is placed at 8 September 2022 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. His accession began a new royal era after seven decades of Elizabethan continuity.
