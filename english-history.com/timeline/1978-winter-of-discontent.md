# Winter of Discontent (1978)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1978-winter-of-discontent
Categories: main-event

Widespread strikes disrupted public and private services during a period of pay restraint and inflation. Within the broader Postwar & Modern UK period, decolonization, welfare institutions, migration, European integration and withdrawal, devolution and social change reshaped the modern United Kingdom. This event is placed at 1978–1979 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The crisis badly damaged the Labour government and helped shift public politics toward the Conservatives.
