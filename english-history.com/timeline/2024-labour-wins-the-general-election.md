# Labour wins the general election (2024)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/2024-labour-wins-the-general-election
Categories: ruler-change, main-event

Labour secured a large Commons majority after fourteen years of Conservative-led government. Within the broader Postwar & Modern UK period, decolonization, welfare institutions, migration, European integration and withdrawal, devolution and social change reshaped the modern United Kingdom. This event is placed at 4 July 2024 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The result marked a decisive electoral realignment and brought Keir Starmer to Downing Street.
