# Roman imperial rule in Britain ends (410)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/410-roman-imperial-rule-in-britain-ends
Categories: main-event

Emperor Honorius told British communities to look to their own defence as Roman administration ceased. Within the broader Roman Britain period, conquest, provincial administration, urbanization, frontier warfare and the gradual weakening of imperial rule reshaped the island. This event is placed at AD 410 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The end of Roman rule opened a new era of local kingdoms, migration and political fragmentation.
