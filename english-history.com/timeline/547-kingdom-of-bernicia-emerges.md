# Kingdom of Bernicia emerges (547)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/547-kingdom-of-bernicia-emerges

Ida is traditionally said to have established the Anglian kingdom of Bernicia in northern Britain. Within the broader Post-Roman Britain period, Roman institutions gave way to competing Brittonic and Anglo-Saxon kingdoms while Christianity was rebuilt through continental and Irish missions. This event is placed at traditional date 547 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. Bernicia later joined Deira to form Northumbria, one of the most powerful early English kingdoms.
