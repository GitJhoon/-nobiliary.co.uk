# Emily Davison dies at the Epsom Derby (1913)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1913-emily-davison-dies-at-the-epsom-derby
Categories: main-event

Suffragette Emily Davison was fatally injured after entering the track in front of the king's horse. Within the broader Early 20th Century & World Wars period, mass democracy, the two world wars, Irish partition, economic crisis and the rise of Labour remade the political and social order. This event is placed at 1913 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. Her death became one of the most famous episodes of the militant suffrage campaign.
