# Liverpool and Manchester Railway opens (1830)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1830-liverpool-and-manchester-railway-opens
Categories: main-event

The world's first major intercity railway opened between two industrial cities. Within the broader Georgian Britain period, parliamentary government, industrialization, imperial expansion, war with France and political reform turned Britain into a global commercial and military power. This event is placed at 1830 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. Its success demonstrated how railways could transform passenger travel, freight and urban growth.
