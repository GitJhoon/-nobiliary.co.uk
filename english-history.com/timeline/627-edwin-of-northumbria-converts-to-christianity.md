# Edwin of Northumbria converts to Christianity (627)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/627-edwin-of-northumbria-converts-to-christianity
Categories: ruler-change

King Edwin was baptized at York under the influence of missionary Paulinus. Within the broader Post-Roman Britain period, Roman institutions gave way to competing Brittonic and Anglo-Saxon kingdoms while Christianity was rebuilt through continental and Irish missions. This event is placed at AD 627 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. His conversion helped establish Christianity in northern England, even though pagan reaction followed his death.
