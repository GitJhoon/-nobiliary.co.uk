# Sutton Hoo ship burial is created (625)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/625-sutton-hoo-ship-burial-is-created
Categories: main-event

The magnificent burial at Sutton Hoo in East Anglia contained weapons, gold and imported luxury goods. Within the broader Post-Roman Britain period, Roman institutions gave way to competing Brittonic and Anglo-Saxon kingdoms while Christianity was rebuilt through continental and Irish missions. This event is placed at c. 625 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. It reveals the wealth, long-distance connections and royal culture of early seventh-century Britain.
