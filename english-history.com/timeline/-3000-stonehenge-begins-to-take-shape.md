# Stonehenge begins to take shape (-3000)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/-3000-stonehenge-begins-to-take-shape
Categories: main-event

Communities on Salisbury Plain began constructing the monument complex that later became Stonehenge. Within the broader Prehistoric Britain period, communities moved from hunter-gatherer lifeways toward farming, monument building, metalworking and increasingly complex regional societies. This event is placed at c. 3000 BCE in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. Its long construction history reflects large-scale cooperation, ritual life and mobility in prehistoric Britain.
