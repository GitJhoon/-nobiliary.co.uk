# Britain declares war on Germany (1939)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1939-britain-declares-war-on-germany
Categories: battle, main-event

Britain entered the Second World War after Germany invaded Poland and ignored an ultimatum to withdraw. Within the broader Early 20th Century & World Wars period, mass democracy, the two world wars, Irish partition, economic crisis and the rise of Labour remade the political and social order. This event is placed at 3 September 1939 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The conflict became a struggle for national survival and reshaped Britain's global position.
