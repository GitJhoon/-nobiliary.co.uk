# Birth of Edward VI (1537)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1537-birth-of-edward-vi

Jane Seymour gave birth to Henry VIII's long-awaited male heir. Within the broader Tudor Britain period, dynastic consolidation, Reformation, closer incorporation of Wales and Ireland, and rivalry with Catholic Europe transformed the state. This event is placed at 1537 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. Edward's birth secured a Tudor succession but his early death later reopened the dynastic question.
