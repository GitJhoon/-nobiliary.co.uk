# Lanfranc becomes Archbishop of Canterbury (1070)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1070-lanfranc-becomes-archbishop-of-canterbury

William appointed Lanfranc to reorganize the English church under Norman leadership. Within the broader Norman & Angevin Britain period, Norman conquest, cross-Channel lordship and royal reform created a new aristocracy and a more centralized monarchy. This event is placed at 1070 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The reform integrated England more closely with continental ecclesiastical networks and replaced many English bishops.
