# Second Reform Act (1867)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1867-second-reform-act
Categories: main-event

Parliament greatly expanded the urban male electorate. Within the broader Victorian Britain period, industrial society, mass politics, public-health reform, global empire and the Irish question dominated public life. This event is placed at 1867 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The act accelerated the development of mass party politics and made working-class voters far more important.
