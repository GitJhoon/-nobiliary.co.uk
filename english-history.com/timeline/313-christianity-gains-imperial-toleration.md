# Christianity gains imperial toleration (313)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/313-christianity-gains-imperial-toleration

The Edict of Milan under Constantine and Licinius legalized Christian worship across the empire. Within the broader Roman Britain period, conquest, provincial administration, urbanization, frontier warfare and the gradual weakening of imperial rule reshaped the island. This event is placed at AD 313 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. British Christian communities subsequently became more visible within the late Roman province.
