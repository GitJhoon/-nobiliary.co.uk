# Battle of Marston Moor (1644)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1644-battle-of-marston-moor
Categories: battle, main-event

Parliamentarian and Scottish forces defeated the Royalists near York. Within the broader Stuarts & Revolutions period, the shared monarchy of England, Scotland and Ireland produced conflicts over religion, taxation and sovereignty that culminated in civil war, revolution and union. This event is placed at 2 July 1644 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The victory gave Parliament control of most of northern England.

## Why it matters
The victory gave Parliament control of most of northern England.
