# Labour wins a landslide under Tony Blair (1997)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1997-labour-wins-a-landslide-under-tony-blair
Categories: ruler-change, main-event

New Labour won a huge parliamentary majority after eighteen years of Conservative government. Within the broader Postwar & Modern UK period, decolonization, welfare institutions, migration, European integration and withdrawal, devolution and social change reshaped the modern United Kingdom. This event is placed at 1 May 1997 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The election opened an era of constitutional reform, public-service investment and a more centrist Labour programme.
