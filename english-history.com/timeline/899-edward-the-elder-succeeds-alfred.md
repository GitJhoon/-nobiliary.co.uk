# Edward the Elder succeeds Alfred (899)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/899-edward-the-elder-succeeds-alfred
Categories: ruler-change

Edward inherited the West Saxon kingdom and continued offensive campaigns against Danish territories. Within the broader Vikings & Unification period, Scandinavian invasion and settlement transformed the political map while Wessex and its allies built the institutions of a unified English kingdom. This event is placed at AD 899 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. His reign turned Alfred's survival strategy into a programme of expansion.
