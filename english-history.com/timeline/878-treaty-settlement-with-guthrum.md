# Treaty settlement with Guthrum (878)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/878-treaty-settlement-with-guthrum
Categories: main-event

Guthrum accepted baptism and a frontier developed between Alfred's realm and Danish-controlled territory. Within the broader Vikings & Unification period, Scandinavian invasion and settlement transformed the political map while Wessex and its allies built the institutions of a unified English kingdom. This event is placed at AD 878 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The settlement laid the foundations for the Danelaw and a divided but more stable England.
