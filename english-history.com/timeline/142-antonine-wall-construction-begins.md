# Antonine Wall construction begins (142)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/142-antonine-wall-construction-begins

Roman forces built a new turf frontier across central Scotland under Antoninus Pius. Within the broader Roman Britain period, conquest, provincial administration, urbanization, frontier warfare and the gradual weakening of imperial rule reshaped the island. This event is placed at AD 142 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The temporary advance shows that the northern frontier remained contested even after Hadrian's Wall.
