# Great Revolt against Henry II (1173)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1173-great-revolt-against-henry-ii
Categories: battle

Henry's sons and several powerful nobles rebelled with support from France and Scotland. Within the broader Norman & Angevin Britain period, Norman conquest, cross-Channel lordship and royal reform created a new aristocracy and a more centralized monarchy. This event is placed at 1173–1174 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. Henry's victory preserved his authority but exposed deep dynastic tensions within the Angevin family.
