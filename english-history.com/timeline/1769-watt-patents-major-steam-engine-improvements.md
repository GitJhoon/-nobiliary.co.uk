# Watt patents major steam-engine improvements (1769)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1769-watt-patents-major-steam-engine-improvements
Categories: main-event

James Watt patented changes that made steam engines much more efficient. Within the broader Georgian Britain period, parliamentary government, industrialization, imperial expansion, war with France and political reform turned Britain into a global commercial and military power. This event is placed at 1769 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. Improved steam power became fundamental to industrial production, mining and transport.
