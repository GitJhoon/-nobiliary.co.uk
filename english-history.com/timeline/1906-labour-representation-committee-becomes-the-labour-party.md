# Labour Representation Committee becomes the Labour Party (1906)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1906-labour-representation-committee-becomes-the-labour-party
Categories: main-event

The emerging labour movement entered Parliament under the Labour Party name. Within the broader Early 20th Century & World Wars period, mass democracy, the two world wars, Irish partition, economic crisis and the rise of Labour remade the political and social order. This event is placed at 1906 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The change marked the rise of a new national party that would eventually replace the Liberals as the main alternative to Conservatives.
