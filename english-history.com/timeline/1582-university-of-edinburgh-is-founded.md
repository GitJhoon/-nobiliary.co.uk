# University of Edinburgh is founded (1582)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1582-university-of-edinburgh-is-founded
Categories: main-event

The town council established a new university with royal approval. Within the broader Tudor Britain period, dynastic consolidation, Reformation, closer incorporation of Wales and Ireland, and rivalry with Catholic Europe transformed the state. This event is placed at 1582 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. It became a major institution of Scottish education and intellectual life.
