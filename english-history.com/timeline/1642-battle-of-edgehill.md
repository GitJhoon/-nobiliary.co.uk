# Battle of Edgehill (1642)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1642-battle-of-edgehill
Categories: battle

Royalist and Parliamentarian armies fought the first major pitched battle of the English Civil War. Within the broader Stuarts & Revolutions period, the shared monarchy of England, Scotland and Ireland produced conflicts over religion, taxation and sovereignty that culminated in civil war, revolution and union. This event is placed at 23 October 1642 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The inconclusive battle showed that neither side could quickly win the conflict.
