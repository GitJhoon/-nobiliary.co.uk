# John Cabot reaches North America (1497)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1497-john-cabot-reaches-north-america
Categories: main-event

Sailing for Henry VII, Cabot reached the North Atlantic coast of North America. Within the broader Tudor Britain period, dynastic consolidation, Reformation, closer incorporation of Wales and Ireland, and rivalry with Catholic Europe transformed the state. This event is placed at 1497 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The voyage became an early basis for later English imperial claims across the Atlantic.
