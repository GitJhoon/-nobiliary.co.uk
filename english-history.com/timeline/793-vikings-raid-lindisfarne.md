# Vikings raid Lindisfarne (793)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/793-vikings-raid-lindisfarne
Categories: main-event

Scandinavian raiders attacked the monastery of Lindisfarne, shocking Christian Europe. Within the broader Vikings & Unification period, Scandinavian invasion and settlement transformed the political map while Wessex and its allies built the institutions of a unified English kingdom. This event is placed at AD 793 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The raid became the conventional opening marker of the Viking Age in Britain.
