# Treaty of Paris recognizes United States independence (1783)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1783-treaty-of-paris-recognizes-united-states-independence
Categories: main-event

Britain formally accepted the independence of the United States. Within the broader Georgian Britain period, parliamentary government, industrialization, imperial expansion, war with France and political reform turned Britain into a global commercial and military power. This event is placed at 1783 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The loss encouraged a reorientation of empire toward Asia, the Pacific and other colonies.
