# Race Relations Act expands anti-discrimination law (1968)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1968-race-relations-act-expands-anti-discrimination-law
Categories: main-event

New legislation extended restrictions on racial discrimination to employment, housing and services. Within the broader Postwar & Modern UK period, decolonization, welfare institutions, migration, European integration and withdrawal, devolution and social change reshaped the modern United Kingdom. This event is placed at 1968 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The act reflected both continuing discrimination and a stronger state commitment to legal remedies.
