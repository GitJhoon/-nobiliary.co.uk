# Referendum confirms European Community membership (1975)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1975-referendum-confirms-european-community-membership
Categories: main-event

Voters chose by a large majority to remain in the European Communities. Within the broader Postwar & Modern UK period, decolonization, welfare institutions, migration, European integration and withdrawal, devolution and social change reshaped the modern United Kingdom. This event is placed at 5 June 1975 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The vote temporarily settled the membership question but did not end political divisions over Europe.
