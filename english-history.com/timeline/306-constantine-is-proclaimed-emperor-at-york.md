# Constantine is proclaimed emperor at York (306)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/306-constantine-is-proclaimed-emperor-at-york
Categories: ruler-change, main-event

After Constantius Chlorus died in York, his troops acclaimed Constantine as emperor. Within the broader Roman Britain period, conquest, provincial administration, urbanization, frontier warfare and the gradual weakening of imperial rule reshaped the island. This event is placed at AD 306 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The proclamation linked Roman Britain directly to the rise of Constantine the Great.
