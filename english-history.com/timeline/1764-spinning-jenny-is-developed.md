# Spinning Jenny is developed (1764)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1764-spinning-jenny-is-developed
Categories: main-event

James Hargreaves's spinning machine greatly increased textile production. Within the broader Georgian Britain period, parliamentary government, industrialization, imperial expansion, war with France and political reform turned Britain into a global commercial and military power. This event is placed at 1764 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. Inventions of this kind helped launch the mechanization at the core of the Industrial Revolution.
