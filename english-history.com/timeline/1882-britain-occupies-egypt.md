# Britain occupies Egypt (1882)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1882-britain-occupies-egypt
Categories: battle, main-event

British forces defeated Egyptian nationalist forces and established effective control while formally leaving Ottoman sovereignty intact. Within the broader Victorian Britain period, industrial society, mass politics, public-health reform, global empire and the Irish question dominated public life. This event is placed at 1882 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The occupation made Egypt and the Suez Canal central to British imperial strategy.
