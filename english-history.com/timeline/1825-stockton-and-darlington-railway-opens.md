# Stockton and Darlington Railway opens (1825)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1825-stockton-and-darlington-railway-opens
Categories: main-event

The railway used steam locomotives to carry freight and passengers. Within the broader Georgian Britain period, parliamentary government, industrialization, imperial expansion, war with France and political reform turned Britain into a global commercial and military power. This event is placed at 1825 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. It demonstrated the commercial potential of rail transport and accelerated a transport revolution.
