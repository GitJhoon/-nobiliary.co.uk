# Great Depression reaches Britain (1929)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1929-great-depression-reaches-britain
Categories: main-event

Global economic collapse intensified unemployment and industrial decline, especially in older manufacturing regions. Within the broader Early 20th Century & World Wars period, mass democracy, the two world wars, Irish partition, economic crisis and the rise of Labour remade the political and social order. This event is placed at 1929 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The crisis reshaped party politics, public spending and debates about economic management.
