# Coronation of Elizabeth II (1953)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1953-coronation-of-elizabeth-ii
Categories: main-event

The coronation at Westminster Abbey was televised to a huge audience. Within the broader Postwar & Modern UK period, decolonization, welfare institutions, migration, European integration and withdrawal, devolution and social change reshaped the modern United Kingdom. This event is placed at 2 June 1953 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The broadcast helped turn the ceremony into a landmark in the rise of mass television culture.
