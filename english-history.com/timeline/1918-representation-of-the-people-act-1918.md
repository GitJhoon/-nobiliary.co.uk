# Representation of the People Act 1918 (1918)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1918-representation-of-the-people-act-1918
Categories: main-event

The electorate was vastly expanded to include nearly all adult men and many women over thirty. Within the broader Early 20th Century & World Wars period, mass democracy, the two world wars, Irish partition, economic crisis and the rise of Labour remade the political and social order. This event is placed at 6 February 1918 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The act transformed Britain into a mass electoral democracy and marked a major victory for women's suffrage.

## Why it matters
