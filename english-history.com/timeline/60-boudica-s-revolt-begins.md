# Boudica's revolt begins (60)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/60-boudica-s-revolt-begins
Categories: battle, main-event

Boudica of the Iceni led a large uprising that destroyed Camulodunum, Londinium and Verulamium. Within the broader Roman Britain period, conquest, provincial administration, urbanization, frontier warfare and the gradual weakening of imperial rule reshaped the island. This event is placed at AD 60 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The revolt exposed the fragility of early Roman rule and became one of the most famous episodes of resistance in British history.
