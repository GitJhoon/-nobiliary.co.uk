# Elizabethan Religious Settlement (1559)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1559-elizabethan-religious-settlement
Categories: main-event

Acts of Supremacy and Uniformity established a Protestant Church of England under royal authority. Within the broader Tudor Britain period, dynastic consolidation, Reformation, closer incorporation of Wales and Ireland, and rivalry with Catholic Europe transformed the state. This event is placed at 1559 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The compromise shaped English religious institutions for generations.

## Why it matters
