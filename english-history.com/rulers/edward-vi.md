# Edward VI - King of England and Ireland

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/edward-vi
Reign: 1547 - 1553
Born-Died: 1537 - 1553
Dynasty: House of Tudor
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_VI

Edward VI became king at nine and ruled through regency governments that pushed the English Reformation decisively in a Protestant direction. His early death reopened the succession struggle and produced the brief attempt to place Lady Jane Grey on the throne.
