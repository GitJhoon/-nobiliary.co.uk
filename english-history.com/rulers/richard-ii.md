# Richard II - King of England

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/richard-ii
Reign: 1377 - 1399
Born-Died: 1367 - 1400
Dynasty: Plantagenets
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_II_of_England

Richard II inherited the throne as a child and confronted the Peasants' Revolt of 1381. His later attempt to establish highly personal royal rule alienated leading nobles and ended with his deposition by Henry Bolingbroke.
