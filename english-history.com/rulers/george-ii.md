# George II - King of Great Britain and Ireland

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/george-ii
Reign: 1727 - 1760
Born-Died: 1683 - 1760
Dynasty: House of Hanover
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_II_of_Great_Britain

George II ruled during a period of expanding British commercial and imperial power. The Jacobite rising of 1745 was defeated, and Britain fought major European and colonial wars that prepared the way for global dominance.
