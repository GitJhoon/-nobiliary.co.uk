# George VI - King of the United Kingdom and the Dominions; last Emperor of India

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/george-vi
Reign: 1936 - 1952
Born-Died: 1895 - 1952
Dynasty: House of Windsor
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_VI

George VI unexpectedly became king after his brother's abdication and served as a symbol of national endurance during the Second World War. His reign also saw the rapid dismantling of empire and the transition toward the modern Commonwealth.

## Succession
- Preceded by: [Edward VIII](https://english-history.com/rulers/edward-viii)
- Succeeded by: [Elizabeth II](https://english-history.com/rulers/elizabeth-ii)
