# Edward the Martyr - King of England

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/edward-the-martyr
Reign: 975 - 978
Born-Died: c. 962 - 978
Dynasty: House of Wessex
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_the_Martyr

Edward succeeded his father Edgar while still young, amid factional rivalry around the royal family. His murder at Corfe in 978 made him a martyr in later English religious tradition.
