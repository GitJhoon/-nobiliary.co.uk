# James II - King of England and Ireland; James VII of Scotland

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/james-ii
Reign: 1685 - 1688
Born-Died: 1633 - 1701
Dynasty: House of Stuart
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_II_of_England

James II openly practiced Catholicism and sought greater religious toleration through expansive use of royal prerogative. The birth of a Catholic male heir intensified opposition, and the Glorious Revolution forced him into exile.
