# Edward the Confessor - King of England

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/edward-the-confessor
Reign: 1042 - 1066
Born-Died: c. 1003 - 1066
Dynasty: House of Wessex
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_the_Confessor

Edward restored the old West Saxon royal house after Danish rule. His childless reign was dominated by the powerful Godwin family and ended in a succession crisis that led directly to the Norman Conquest.

## Succession
- Preceded by: [Harthacnut](https://english-history.com/rulers/harthacnut)
- Succeeded by: [Harold II](https://english-history.com/rulers/harold-ii)
