# Alfred the Great - King of Wessex

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/alfred-the-great
Reign: 871 - 899
Born-Died: 849 - 899
Dynasty: House of Wessex
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_the_Great

Alfred became the central figure in Anglo-Saxon resistance to Viking conquest. After surviving the crisis of 878, he rebuilt Wessex through fortified towns, military reform, law and an ambitious programme of learning and translation.
