# William IV - King of the United Kingdom and Hanover

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/william-iv
Reign: 1830 - 1837
Born-Died: 1765 - 1837
Dynasty: House of Hanover
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_IV

William IV's reign coincided with a major transformation of the British political system. The Reform Act 1832 widened parliamentary representation, while slavery was abolished across most of the British Empire.
