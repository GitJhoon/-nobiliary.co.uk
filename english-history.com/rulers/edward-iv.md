# Edward IV - King of England

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/edward-iv
Reign: 1461 - 1470; 1471 - 1483
Born-Died: 1442 - 1483
Dynasty: House of York
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_IV_of_England

Edward IV seized the throne for the Yorkist cause during the Wars of the Roses. After a brief exile and restoration of Henry VI, Edward returned in 1471 and ruled with increasing stability until his sudden death.
