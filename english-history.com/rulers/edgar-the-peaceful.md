# Edgar the Peaceful - King of England

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/edgar-the-peaceful
Reign: 959 - 975
Born-Died: c. 943 - 975
Dynasty: House of Wessex
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edgar,_King_of_England

Edgar reunited England and presided over a period of unusual internal stability. His government supported monastic reform and strengthened the institutions of a single English kingdom.
