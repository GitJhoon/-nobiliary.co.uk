# Edward III - King of England

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/edward-iii
Reign: 1327 - 1377
Born-Died: 1312 - 1377
Dynasty: Plantagenets
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_III_of_England

Edward III restored royal authority after his father's deposition and launched the Hundred Years' War by pressing a claim to the French crown. Victories at Crécy and Poitiers made his reign a high point of medieval English military prestige.

- Preceded by: [Edward II](https://english-history.com/rulers/edward-ii)
