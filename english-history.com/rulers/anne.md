# Anne - Queen of England, Scotland and Ireland; from 1707 Queen of Great Britain

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/anne
Reign: 1702 - 1714
Born-Died: 1665 - 1714
Dynasty: House of Stuart
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anne,_Queen_of_Great_Britain

Anne was the last Stuart monarch and the sovereign under whom England and Scotland were politically united. The Acts of Union took effect in 1707, creating the Kingdom of Great Britain and a single Parliament at Westminster.
