# Henry II - King of England

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/henry-ii
Reign: 1154 - 1189
Born-Died: 1133 - 1189
Dynasty: Plantagenets
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_II_of_England

Henry II founded the Plantagenet dynasty and ruled a vast collection of territories later called the Angevin Empire. He strengthened royal justice and administration but became locked in conflicts with Thomas Becket and with his own sons.
