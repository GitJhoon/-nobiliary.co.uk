# John - King of England

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/john
Reign: 1199 - 1216
Born-Died: 1166 - 1216
Dynasty: Plantagenets
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John,_King_of_England

John lost most of the Plantagenet lands in France and faced a rebellion by English barons. The resulting crisis forced him to seal Magna Carta in 1215, one of the foundational documents in the later history of constitutional government.

## Succession
- Preceded by: [Richard I](https://english-history.com/rulers/richard-i)
- Succeeded by: [Henry III](https://english-history.com/rulers/henry-iii)
