# Elizabeth II - Queen of the United Kingdom and other Commonwealth realms

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/elizabeth-ii
Reign: 1952 - 2022
Born-Died: 1926 - 2022
Dynasty: House of Windsor
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_II

Elizabeth II became queen in 1952 and reigned for more than seventy years, the longest reign in British history. Her constitutional role remained formally limited, but she became a central symbol of continuity as Britain, the Commonwealth and society changed profoundly.

- Preceded by: [George VI](https://english-history.com/rulers/george-vi)
- Succeeded by: [Charles III](https://english-history.com/rulers/charles-iii)
