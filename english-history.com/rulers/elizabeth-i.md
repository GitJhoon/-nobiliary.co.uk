# Elizabeth I - Queen of England and Ireland

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/elizabeth-i
Reign: 1558 - 1603
Born-Died: 1533 - 1603
Dynasty: House of Tudor
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_I

Elizabeth I restored a Protestant religious settlement and presided over a period of growing maritime power, overseas ventures and cultural achievement. Her defeat of the Spanish Armada became a defining symbol of the reign, but she died childless and ended the Tudor dynasty.
