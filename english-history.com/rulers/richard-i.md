# Richard I - King of England

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/richard-i
Reign: 1189 - 1199
Born-Died: 1157 - 1199
Dynasty: Plantagenets
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_I_of_England

Richard the Lionheart spent much of his reign abroad on crusade, in captivity or defending his continental lands. His military reputation became legendary, though the costs of warfare placed heavy demands on England.
