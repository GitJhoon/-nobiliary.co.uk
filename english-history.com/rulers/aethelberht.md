# Æthelberht - King of Wessex

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/aethelberht
Reign: 860 - 866
Born-Died: c. 835 - 865
Dynasty: House of Wessex
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%86thelberht,_King_of_Wessex

Æthelberht reunited territories previously divided among members of the West Saxon royal family. His reign coincided with increasingly frequent Viking raids but preserved dynastic continuity.

## Succession
- Preceded by: [Æthelbald](https://english-history.com/rulers/aethelbald)
- Succeeded by: [Æthelred I](https://english-history.com/rulers/aethelred-i)
