# William I - King of England

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/william-i
Reign: 1066 - 1087
Born-Died: c. 1028 - 1087
Dynasty: Norman Dynasty
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_the_Conqueror

William, Duke of Normandy, conquered England in 1066 and replaced much of the Anglo-Saxon elite with a Norman ruling class. His reign transformed landholding, castle-building, aristocratic culture and the relationship between England and continental Europe.
