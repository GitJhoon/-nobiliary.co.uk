# George V - King of the United Kingdom and the British Dominions; Emperor of India

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/george-v
Reign: 1910 - 1936
Born-Died: 1865 - 1936
Dynasty: House of Windsor
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_V

George V's reign encompassed the First World War, revolution across Europe and major constitutional change within the British Empire. In 1917 he changed the royal house's name from Saxe-Coburg and Gotha to Windsor in response to wartime anti-German sentiment.
