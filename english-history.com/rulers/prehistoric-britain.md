# Prehistoric Britain - No single ruler

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/prehistoric-britain
Reign: c. 800,000 BCE - AD 43
Dynasty: Prehistoric Britain
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prehistoric_Britain

For most of prehistory Britain had no state, crown, or single ruler. Communities ranged from small hunter-gatherer groups to farming societies, tribal confederations and Iron Age chiefdoms whose authority was local and often temporary.
