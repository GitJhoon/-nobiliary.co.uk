# Edward I - King of England

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/edward-i
Reign: 1272 - 1307
Born-Died: 1239 - 1307
Dynasty: Plantagenets
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_I_of_England

Edward I strengthened royal law and Parliament while pursuing aggressive conquest in Wales and war in Scotland. His reign permanently reshaped the political map of Britain but also imposed heavy fiscal and military burdens.

## Succession
- Preceded by: [Henry III](https://english-history.com/rulers/henry-iii)
- Succeeded by: [Edward II](https://english-history.com/rulers/edward-ii)
