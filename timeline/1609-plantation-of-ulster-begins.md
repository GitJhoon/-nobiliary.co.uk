# Plantation of Ulster begins (1609)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1609-plantation-of-ulster-begins
Categories: main-event

The crown confiscated large areas in Ulster and settled English and Scottish Protestants. Within the broader Stuarts & Revolutions period, the shared monarchy of England, Scotland and Ireland produced conflicts over religion, taxation and sovereignty that culminated in civil war, revolution and union. This event is placed at 1609 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The plantation profoundly altered landholding, religion and politics in Ireland and influenced later conflict.
