# Norman castle building accelerates (1067)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1067-norman-castle-building-accelerates

William's followers constructed motte-and-bailey castles across strategic centres. Within the broader Norman & Angevin Britain period, Norman conquest, cross-Channel lordship and royal reform created a new aristocracy and a more centralized monarchy. This event is placed at 1067 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The castles helped a small conquering elite dominate towns, roads and rebellious regions.
