# French Revolution transforms British politics (1789)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1789-french-revolution-transforms-british-politics
Categories: main-event

Events in France provoked intense debate in Britain over reform, monarchy and popular rights. Within the broader Georgian Britain period, parliamentary government, industrialization, imperial expansion, war with France and political reform turned Britain into a global commercial and military power. This event is placed at 1789 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The revolution polarized political culture and shaped domestic repression and reform for decades.
