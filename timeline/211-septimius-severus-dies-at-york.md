# Septimius Severus dies at York (211)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/211-septimius-severus-dies-at-york
Categories: ruler-change

The emperor died at Eboracum while directing northern operations. Within the broader Roman Britain period, conquest, provincial administration, urbanization, frontier warfare and the gradual weakening of imperial rule reshaped the island. This event is placed at AD 211 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. His death ended the last major imperial attempt to conquer Caledonia on a large scale.
