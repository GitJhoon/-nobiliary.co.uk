# Poynings' Law is enacted in Ireland (1494)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1494-poynings-law-is-enacted-in-ireland
Categories: main-event

The Irish Parliament was placed under tighter control of the English crown and council. Within the broader Tudor Britain period, dynastic consolidation, Reformation, closer incorporation of Wales and Ireland, and rivalry with Catholic Europe transformed the state. This event is placed at 1494 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The measure became a long-lasting instrument of English constitutional authority in Ireland.
