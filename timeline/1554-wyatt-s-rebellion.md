# Wyatt's Rebellion (1554)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1554-wyatt-s-rebellion

Thomas Wyatt led a rising against Mary's planned marriage to Philip of Spain. Within the broader Tudor Britain period, dynastic consolidation, Reformation, closer incorporation of Wales and Ireland, and rivalry with Catholic Europe transformed the state. This event is placed at 1554 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The revolt failed but deepened the regime's suspicion of Protestant opponents and Elizabeth.
