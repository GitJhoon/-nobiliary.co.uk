# Gunpowder Plot is uncovered (1605)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1605-gunpowder-plot-is-uncovered
Categories: main-event

Catholic conspirators planned to destroy the king and Parliament with explosives beneath the House of Lords. Within the broader Stuarts & Revolutions period, the shared monarchy of England, Scotland and Ireland produced conflicts over religion, taxation and sovereignty that culminated in civil war, revolution and union. This event is placed at 5 November 1605 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The failed plot became a lasting symbol of religious conflict and state security.
