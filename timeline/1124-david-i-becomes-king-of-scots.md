# David I becomes king of Scots (1124)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1124-david-i-becomes-king-of-scots
Categories: ruler-change, main-event

David succeeded to the Scottish throne after years spent at the Anglo-Norman court. Within the broader Norman & Angevin Britain period, Norman conquest, cross-Channel lordship and royal reform created a new aristocracy and a more centralized monarchy. This event is placed at 1124 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. His reign encouraged major administrative, ecclesiastical and feudal changes often called the Davidian Revolution.
