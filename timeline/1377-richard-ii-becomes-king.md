# Richard II becomes king (1377)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1377-richard-ii-becomes-king
Categories: ruler-change

The ten-year-old Richard succeeded Edward III. Within the broader Medieval Kingdoms period, the English, Scottish and Welsh polities were reshaped by conquest, parliamentary development, dynastic conflict, plague and long wars with France. This event is placed at 1377 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. His minority and later personal rule produced deep conflict with magnates and Parliament.
