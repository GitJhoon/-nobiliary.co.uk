# Calais falls to France (1558)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1558-calais-falls-to-france
Categories: main-event

French forces captured England's last major territorial possession on the continent. Within the broader Tudor Britain period, dynastic consolidation, Reformation, closer incorporation of Wales and Ireland, and rivalry with Catholic Europe transformed the state. This event is placed at January 1558 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The loss symbolized the end of a long medieval English presence in France.
