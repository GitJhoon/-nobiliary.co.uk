# Spithead and Nore mutinies (1797)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1797-spithead-and-nore-mutinies
Categories: main-event

Royal Navy sailors mutinied over pay, conditions and discipline. Within the broader Georgian Britain period, parliamentary government, industrialization, imperial expansion, war with France and political reform turned Britain into a global commercial and military power. This event is placed at 1797 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The crisis showed both the navy's grievances and its central importance during wartime.
