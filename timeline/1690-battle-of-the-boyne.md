# Battle of the Boyne (1690)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1690-battle-of-the-boyne
Categories: battle, main-event

William III defeated James II's army in Ireland. Within the broader Stuarts & Revolutions period, the shared monarchy of England, Scotland and Ireland produced conflicts over religion, taxation and sovereignty that culminated in civil war, revolution and union. This event is placed at 1 July 1690 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The battle became a central event in the Williamite war and later unionist commemorative tradition.
