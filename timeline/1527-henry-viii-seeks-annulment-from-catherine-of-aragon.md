# Henry VIII seeks annulment from Catherine of Aragon (1527)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1527-henry-viii-seeks-annulment-from-catherine-of-aragon
Categories: main-event

Henry pursued an end to his marriage because it had produced no surviving male heir. Within the broader Tudor Britain period, dynastic consolidation, Reformation, closer incorporation of Wales and Ireland, and rivalry with Catholic Europe transformed the state. This event is placed at 1527 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The unresolved 'King's Great Matter' drove the crown toward a break with papal authority.
