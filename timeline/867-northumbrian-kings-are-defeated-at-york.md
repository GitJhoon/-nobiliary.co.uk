# Northumbrian kings are defeated at York (867)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/867-northumbrian-kings-are-defeated-at-york
Categories: battle, ruler-change

Rival Northumbrian kings Ælla and Osberht were killed while trying to retake York. Within the broader Vikings & Unification period, Scandinavian invasion and settlement transformed the political map while Wessex and its allies built the institutions of a unified English kingdom. This event is placed at AD 867 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. Their deaths ended independent Anglo-Saxon rule in much of Northumbria.

## Why it matters
Their deaths ended independent Anglo-Saxon rule in much of Northumbria.

Ruler at the time: [Æthelred I](https://english-history.com/rulers/aethelred-i)
