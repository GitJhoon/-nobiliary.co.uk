# Julius Caesar's first expedition to Britain (-55)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/-55-julius-caesar-s-first-expedition-to-britain
Categories: main-event

Julius Caesar crossed the Channel with Roman forces but withdrew after a limited campaign in south-eastern Britain. Within the broader Roman Britain period, conquest, provincial administration, urbanization, frontier warfare and the gradual weakening of imperial rule reshaped the island. This event is placed at 55 BCE in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The expedition brought Britain firmly into Roman political awareness and written history.
