# Æthelflæd becomes Lady of the Mercians (911)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/911-aethelflaed-becomes-lady-of-the-mercians
Categories: ruler-change, main-event

After her husband Æthelred's death, Alfred's daughter Æthelflæd ruled Mercia. Within the broader Vikings & Unification period, Scandinavian invasion and settlement transformed the political map while Wessex and its allies built the institutions of a unified English kingdom. This event is placed at AD 911 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. Her leadership was crucial to the conquest of Danish-held towns and to cooperation between Mercia and Wessex.
