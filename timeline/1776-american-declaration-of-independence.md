# American Declaration of Independence (1776)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1776-american-declaration-of-independence
Categories: main-event

The thirteen colonies declared themselves independent from Britain. Within the broader Georgian Britain period, parliamentary government, industrialization, imperial expansion, war with France and political reform turned Britain into a global commercial and military power. This event is placed at 4 July 1776 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The declaration transformed the imperial war into a struggle over the creation of a new sovereign state.

## Why it matters
The declaration transformed the imperial war into a struggle over the creation of a new sovereign state.
