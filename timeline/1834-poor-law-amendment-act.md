# Poor Law Amendment Act (1834)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1834-poor-law-amendment-act
Categories: main-event

A new national system emphasized workhouses and stricter relief for the poor. Within the broader Georgian Britain period, parliamentary government, industrialization, imperial expansion, war with France and political reform turned Britain into a global commercial and military power. This event is placed at 1834 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The reform became one of the most controversial social policies of nineteenth-century Britain.

## Why it matters
