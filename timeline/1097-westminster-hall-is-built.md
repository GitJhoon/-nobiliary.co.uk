# Westminster Hall is built (1097)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1097-westminster-hall-is-built
Categories: main-event

William II constructed the great hall at Westminster Palace. Within the broader Norman & Angevin Britain period, Norman conquest, cross-Channel lordship and royal reform created a new aristocracy and a more centralized monarchy. This event is placed at 1097 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. It became a lasting centre of royal ceremony, law and later parliamentary life.
