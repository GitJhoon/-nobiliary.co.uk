# Elizabeth II becomes queen (1952)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1952-elizabeth-ii-becomes-queen
Categories: ruler-change, main-event

Elizabeth succeeded George VI while on a Commonwealth tour in Kenya. Within the broader Postwar & Modern UK period, decolonization, welfare institutions, migration, European integration and withdrawal, devolution and social change reshaped the modern United Kingdom. This event is placed at 6 February 1952 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. Her seventy-year reign spanned decolonization, social transformation and repeated constitutional change.
