# Battle of Culloden (1746)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1746-battle-of-culloden
Categories: battle, main-event

Government troops decisively defeated the Jacobite army near Inverness. Within the broader Georgian Britain period, parliamentary government, industrialization, imperial expansion, war with France and political reform turned Britain into a global commercial and military power. This event is placed at 16 April 1746 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The battle ended the last major armed attempt to restore the Stuarts and was followed by harsh repression in the Highlands.
