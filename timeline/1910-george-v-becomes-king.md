# George V becomes king (1910)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1910-george-v-becomes-king
Categories: ruler-change

George V succeeded Edward VII amid constitutional conflict over the House of Lords. Within the broader Early 20th Century & World Wars period, mass democracy, the two world wars, Irish partition, economic crisis and the rise of Labour remade the political and social order. This event is placed at 6 May 1910 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. His reign spanned the First World War, Irish independence and the emergence of mass democracy.
