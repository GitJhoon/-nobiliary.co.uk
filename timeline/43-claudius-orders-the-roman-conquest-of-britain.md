# Claudius orders the Roman conquest of Britain (43)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/43-claudius-orders-the-roman-conquest-of-britain
Categories: battle, main-event

Emperor Claudius sent a major invasion force under Aulus Plautius, beginning permanent Roman conquest. Within the broader Roman Britain period, conquest, provincial administration, urbanization, frontier warfare and the gradual weakening of imperial rule reshaped the island. This event is placed at AD 43 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The invasion transformed Britain into a province of the Roman Empire and reshaped its towns, economy and politics.
