# Government of India Act transfers rule to the Crown (1858)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1858-government-of-india-act-transfers-rule-to-the-crown
Categories: main-event

The British state replaced the East India Company as the supreme authority in India. Within the broader Victorian Britain period, industrial society, mass politics, public-health reform, global empire and the Irish question dominated public life. This event is placed at 1858 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The change began the period commonly known as the British Raj.

## Why it matters
The change began the period commonly known as the British Raj.

Ruler at the time: [Victoria](https://english-history.com/rulers/victoria)
