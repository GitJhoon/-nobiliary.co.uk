# Henry I becomes king (1100)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1100-henry-i-becomes-king
Categories: ruler-change

Henry seized the English crown after William II died in a hunting accident. Within the broader Norman & Angevin Britain period, Norman conquest, cross-Channel lordship and royal reform created a new aristocracy and a more centralized monarchy. This event is placed at 1100 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. His accession began a reign noted for administrative consolidation and royal justice.
