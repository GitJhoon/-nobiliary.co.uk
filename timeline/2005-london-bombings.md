# London bombings (2005)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/2005-london-bombings
Categories: main-event

Four suicide bombers attacked public transport, killing fifty-two victims. Within the broader Postwar & Modern UK period, decolonization, welfare institutions, migration, European integration and withdrawal, devolution and social change reshaped the modern United Kingdom. This event is placed at 7 July 2005 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The attacks transformed domestic counter-terrorism policy and public debate over security and extremism.
