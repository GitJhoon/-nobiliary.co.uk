# Claim of Right in Scotland (1689)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1689-claim-of-right-in-scotland
Categories: main-event

The Scottish Convention declared that James VII had forfeited the crown and offered it to William and Mary. Within the broader Stuarts & Revolutions period, the shared monarchy of England, Scotland and Ireland produced conflicts over religion, taxation and sovereignty that culminated in civil war, revolution and union. This event is placed at 1689 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The settlement reshaped Scottish monarchy on constitutional and Presbyterian terms.

## Why it matters
The settlement reshaped Scottish monarchy on constitutional and Presbyterian terms.
