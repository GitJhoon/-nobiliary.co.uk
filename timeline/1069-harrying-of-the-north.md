# Harrying of the North (1069)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1069-harrying-of-the-north
Categories: battle, main-event

William devastated large areas of northern England after repeated rebellions and Danish intervention. Within the broader Norman & Angevin Britain period, Norman conquest, cross-Channel lordship and royal reform created a new aristocracy and a more centralized monarchy. This event is placed at 1069–1070 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The campaign caused severe destruction and became one of the harshest episodes of the conquest.
