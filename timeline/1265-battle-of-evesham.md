# Battle of Evesham (1265)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1265-battle-of-evesham
Categories: battle

Prince Edward defeated and killed Simon de Montfort. Within the broader Medieval Kingdoms period, the English, Scottish and Welsh polities were reshaped by conquest, parliamentary development, dynastic conflict, plague and long wars with France. This event is placed at 4 August 1265 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The battle restored royal authority but did not erase the political innovations of the reform era.
