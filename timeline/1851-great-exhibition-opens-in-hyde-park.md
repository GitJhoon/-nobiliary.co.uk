# Great Exhibition opens in Hyde Park (1851)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1851-great-exhibition-opens-in-hyde-park
Categories: main-event

The Crystal Palace displayed industrial products and art from Britain and around the world. Within the broader Victorian Britain period, industrial society, mass politics, public-health reform, global empire and the Irish question dominated public life. This event is placed at 1851 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The exhibition celebrated manufacturing, commerce and imperial confidence at mid-century.
