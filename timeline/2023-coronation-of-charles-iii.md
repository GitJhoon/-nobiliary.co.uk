# Coronation of Charles III (2023)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/2023-coronation-of-charles-iii
Categories: main-event

Charles III and Queen Camilla were crowned at Westminster Abbey. Within the broader Postwar & Modern UK period, decolonization, welfare institutions, migration, European integration and withdrawal, devolution and social change reshaped the modern United Kingdom. This event is placed at 6 May 2023 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The ceremony combined ancient constitutional ritual with a modern, diverse United Kingdom.
