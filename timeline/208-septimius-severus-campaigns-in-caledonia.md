# Septimius Severus campaigns in Caledonia (208)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/208-septimius-severus-campaigns-in-caledonia
Categories: battle

Emperor Septimius Severus personally led a large army into northern Scotland. Within the broader Roman Britain period, conquest, provincial administration, urbanization, frontier warfare and the gradual weakening of imperial rule reshaped the island. This event is placed at AD 208 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The costly campaigns demonstrated Rome's continuing inability to impose lasting rule across all of Britain.
