# Scottish Prayer Book crisis (1637)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1637-scottish-prayer-book-crisis
Categories: main-event

Attempts to impose a new prayer book provoked riots and organized opposition in Scotland. Within the broader Stuarts & Revolutions period, the shared monarchy of England, Scotland and Ireland produced conflicts over religion, taxation and sovereignty that culminated in civil war, revolution and union. This event is placed at 1637 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The crisis launched the Covenanting movement and directly destabilized Charles I's kingdoms.
