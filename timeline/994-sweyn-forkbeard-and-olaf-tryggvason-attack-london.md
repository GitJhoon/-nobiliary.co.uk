# Sweyn Forkbeard and Olaf Tryggvason attack London (994)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/994-sweyn-forkbeard-and-olaf-tryggvason-attack-london
Categories: battle

A major Scandinavian fleet attacked England and extracted payment. Within the broader Vikings & Unification period, Scandinavian invasion and settlement transformed the political map while Wessex and its allies built the institutions of a unified English kingdom. This event is placed at AD 994 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The campaign showed the growing inability of Æthelred's government to deter Viking armies.

## Why it matters
