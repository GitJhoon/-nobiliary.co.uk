# Trade Union Act gives unions legal recognition (1871)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1871-trade-union-act-gives-unions-legal-recognition
Categories: main-event

Parliament clarified the lawful status of trade unions and their funds. Within the broader Victorian Britain period, industrial society, mass politics, public-health reform, global empire and the Irish question dominated public life. This event is placed at 1871 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The reform strengthened organized labour as a permanent part of British society.

## Why it matters
