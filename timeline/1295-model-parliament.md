# Model Parliament (1295)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1295-model-parliament
Categories: main-event

Edward I summoned clergy, nobles, knights and borough representatives to approve taxation. Within the broader Medieval Kingdoms period, the English, Scottish and Welsh polities were reshaped by conquest, parliamentary development, dynastic conflict, plague and long wars with France. This event is placed at 1295 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The assembly became an influential model for the later composition of Parliament.

## Why it matters
