# Corn Laws are repealed (1846)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1846-corn-laws-are-repealed
Categories: main-event

Robert Peel's government ended the protective grain tariffs. Within the broader Victorian Britain period, industrial society, mass politics, public-health reform, global empire and the Irish question dominated public life. This event is placed at 1846 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. Repeal marked a decisive victory for free-trade politics and split the Conservative Party.
