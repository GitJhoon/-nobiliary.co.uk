# English invasion of France captures Boulogne (1544)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1544-english-invasion-of-france-captures-boulogne
Categories: battle

Henry VIII personally campaigned in France and took Boulogne. Within the broader Tudor Britain period, dynastic consolidation, Reformation, closer incorporation of Wales and Ireland, and rivalry with Catholic Europe transformed the state. This event is placed at 1544 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The costly war delivered prestige but strained royal finances and ended with limited lasting gains.
