# Pope Pius V excommunicates Elizabeth I (1570)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1570-pope-pius-v-excommunicates-elizabeth-i
Categories: main-event

The papal bull Regnans in Excelsis declared Elizabeth deposed. Within the broader Tudor Britain period, dynastic consolidation, Reformation, closer incorporation of Wales and Ireland, and rivalry with Catholic Europe transformed the state. This event is placed at 1570 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The act sharpened the political association between Catholic dissent and treason in Elizabethan England.
