# Owain Glyndŵr rebellion begins (1400)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1400-owain-glynd-r-rebellion-begins
Categories: main-event

Owain Glyndŵr led a major Welsh revolt against English rule. Within the broader Medieval Kingdoms period, the English, Scottish and Welsh polities were reshaped by conquest, parliamentary development, dynastic conflict, plague and long wars with France. This event is placed at 1400 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The uprising briefly created an alternative Welsh government and became a lasting symbol of Welsh national resistance.

## Why it matters
