# Reformation Parliament begins (1529)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1529-reformation-parliament-begins
Categories: main-event

Henry VIII summoned the Parliament that would pass a series of statutes dismantling papal jurisdiction. Within the broader Tudor Britain period, dynastic consolidation, Reformation, closer incorporation of Wales and Ireland, and rivalry with Catholic Europe transformed the state. This event is placed at 1529 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. Its legislation fundamentally expanded the role of statute and crown in English religious government.

## Why it matters
