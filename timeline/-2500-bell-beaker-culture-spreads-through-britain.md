# Bell Beaker culture spreads through Britain (-2500)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/-2500-bell-beaker-culture-spreads-through-britain

New burial customs, metalworking traditions and genetic ancestry associated with Bell Beaker populations expanded rapidly. Within the broader Prehistoric Britain period, communities moved from hunter-gatherer lifeways toward farming, monument building, metalworking and increasingly complex regional societies. This event is placed at c. 2500 BCE in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The change marked one of the most significant demographic and cultural turnovers in British prehistory.
