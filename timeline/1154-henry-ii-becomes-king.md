# Henry II becomes king (1154)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1154-henry-ii-becomes-king
Categories: ruler-change, main-event

Henry of Anjou succeeded Stephen and founded the Angevin, or Plantagenet, dynasty. Within the broader Norman & Angevin Britain period, Norman conquest, cross-Channel lordship and royal reform created a new aristocracy and a more centralized monarchy. This event is placed at 1154 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. His vast continental possessions and energetic government reshaped English monarchy.

## Why it matters
