# Habeas corpus is suspended amid radical fears (1794)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1794-habeas-corpus-is-suspended-amid-radical-fears
Categories: main-event

The government detained reformers and tightened controls on political organization. Within the broader Georgian Britain period, parliamentary government, industrialization, imperial expansion, war with France and political reform turned Britain into a global commercial and military power. This event is placed at 1794 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The measures reflected elite fears that the French Revolution could inspire upheaval in Britain.
