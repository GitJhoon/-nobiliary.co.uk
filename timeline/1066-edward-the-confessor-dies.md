# Edward the Confessor dies (1066)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1066-edward-the-confessor-dies
Categories: ruler-change, main-event

Edward died without a direct heir, leaving several claimants to the English throne. Within the broader Vikings & Unification period, Scandinavian invasion and settlement transformed the political map while Wessex and its allies built the institutions of a unified English kingdom. This event is placed at 5 January 1066 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. The succession crisis immediately triggered the most famous year in medieval English history.

## Why it matters
The succession crisis immediately triggered the most famous year in medieval English history.

Ruler at the time: [William I](https://english-history.com/rulers/william-i)
