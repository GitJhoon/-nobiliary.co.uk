# Union of the Crowns (1603)

URL: https://english-history.com/timeline/1603-union-of-the-crowns
Categories: ruler-change, main-event

James VI of Scotland inherited the English and Irish crowns as James I. Within the broader Stuarts & Revolutions period, the shared monarchy of England, Scotland and Ireland produced conflicts over religion, taxation and sovereignty that culminated in civil war, revolution and union. This event is placed at 24 March 1603 in the chronology so it can be read alongside the political, military, social and cultural changes around it. England and Scotland kept separate parliaments and laws, but shared monarchy created a new framework for British politics.
