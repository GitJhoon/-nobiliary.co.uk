# Commonwealth of England - Republican government

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/commonwealth-of-england
Reign: 1649 - 1653
Dynasty: Commonwealth
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commonwealth_of_England

After Charles I's execution, the monarchy and House of Lords were abolished and England was declared a Commonwealth. Power lay formally with the Rump Parliament and Council of State, while the army and Oliver Cromwell became increasingly dominant.

- Preceded by: [Charles I](https://english-history.com/rulers/charles-i)
