# Lady Jane Grey - Disputed Queen of England and Ireland

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/lady-jane-grey
Reign: 10 - 19 July 1553
Born-Died: 1537 - 1554
Dynasty: Tudor Claimant
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Jane_Grey

Jane Grey was proclaimed queen under Edward VI's succession plan but lacked broad political support. Mary Tudor rapidly gathered backing, entered London and displaced Jane after nine days.
