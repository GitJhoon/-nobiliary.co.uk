# Henry VIII - King of England

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/henry-viii
Reign: 1509 - 1547
Born-Died: 1491 - 1547
Dynasty: House of Tudor
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_VIII

Henry VIII transformed the English monarchy by breaking with papal authority and establishing royal supremacy over the Church of England. His six marriages, dynastic anxieties and dissolution of the monasteries made his reign a defining turning point.
