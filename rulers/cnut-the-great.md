# Cnut the Great - King of England

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/cnut-the-great
Reign: 1016 - 1035
Born-Died: c. 990 - 1035
Dynasty: House of Denmark
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cnut

Cnut conquered England and built a North Sea empire that also encompassed Denmark and Norway. He governed through a blend of Scandinavian power and English institutions, presenting himself as a Christian European king.
