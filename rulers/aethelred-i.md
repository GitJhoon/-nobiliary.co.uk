# Æthelred I - King of Wessex

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/aethelred-i
Reign: 866 - 871
Born-Died: c. 847 - 871
Dynasty: House of Wessex
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%86thelred_I,_King_of_Wessex

Æthelred I ruled as the Great Heathen Army overran much of Anglo-Saxon England. He and his younger brother Alfred fought repeated campaigns to keep Wessex independent.

## Succession
- Preceded by: [Æthelberht](https://english-history.com/rulers/aethelberht)
- Succeeded by: [Alfred the Great](https://english-history.com/rulers/alfred-the-great)
