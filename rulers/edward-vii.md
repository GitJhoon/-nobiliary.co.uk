# Edward VII - King of the United Kingdom and British Dominions; Emperor of India

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/edward-vii
Reign: 1901 - 1910
Born-Died: 1841 - 1910
Dynasty: Saxe-Coburg and Gotha
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_VII

Edward VII succeeded Victoria after decades as Prince of Wales. His short reign coincided with social change, naval rivalry and diplomatic realignments that produced the Entente Cordiale with France.

## Succession
- Preceded by: [Victoria](https://english-history.com/rulers/victoria)
- Succeeded by: [George V](https://english-history.com/rulers/george-v)
