# Henry VII - King of England

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/henry-vii
Reign: 1485 - 1509
Born-Died: 1457 - 1509
Dynasty: House of Tudor
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_VII_of_England

Henry Tudor defeated Richard III at Bosworth and founded the Tudor dynasty. He ended major dynastic warfare, strengthened royal finances and used marriage diplomacy to secure the new regime.

## Succession
- Preceded by: [Richard III](https://english-history.com/rulers/richard-iii)
- Succeeded by: [Henry VIII](https://english-history.com/rulers/henry-viii)
