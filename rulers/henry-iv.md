# Henry IV - King of England

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/henry-iv
Reign: 1399 - 1413
Born-Died: 1367 - 1413
Dynasty: House of Lancaster
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_IV_of_England

Henry Bolingbroke overthrew Richard II and founded the Lancastrian royal line. His reign was marked by legitimacy problems, noble rebellions and conflict in Wales led by Owain Glyndŵr.

## Succession
- Preceded by: [Richard II](https://english-history.com/rulers/richard-ii)
- Succeeded by: [Henry V](https://english-history.com/rulers/henry-v)
