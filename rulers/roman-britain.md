# Roman Britain - Roman imperial administration

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/roman-britain
Reign: 43 - c. 410
Dynasty: Roman Britain
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Britain

After the Claudian invasion of AD 43, much of Britain became part of the Roman Empire and was governed through emperors, provincial governors, military commanders and civic institutions rather than a native British monarchy. Roman authority expanded, contracted and was reorganized several times before imperial government withdrew in the early fifth century.
