# George III - King of Great Britain and Ireland; from 1801 King of the United Kingdom

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/george-iii
Reign: 1760 - 1820
Born-Died: 1738 - 1820
Dynasty: House of Hanover
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_III

George III's exceptionally long reign spanned the loss of the American colonies, wars with Revolutionary and Napoleonic France, and the union with Ireland in 1801. Recurrent illness eventually led to the Regency under his son.
