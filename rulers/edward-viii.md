# Edward VIII - King of the United Kingdom and the Dominions; Emperor of India

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/edward-viii
Reign: January - December 1936
Born-Died: 1894 - 1972
Dynasty: House of Windsor
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_VIII

Edward VIII's reign lasted less than a year. His determination to marry Wallis Simpson, a divorced American, produced a constitutional crisis that ended with his abdication in favour of his brother.
