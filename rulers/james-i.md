# James I - King of England and Ireland; James VI of Scotland

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/james-i
Reign: 1603 - 1625
Born-Died: 1566 - 1625
Dynasty: House of Stuart
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_VI_and_I

James VI of Scotland inherited the English throne in 1603, creating the Union of the Crowns under one monarch while England and Scotland remained separate states. He promoted a stronger theory of monarchy, sponsored the King James Bible and repeatedly clashed with Parliament over finance and prerogative.
