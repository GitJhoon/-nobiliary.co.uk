# Charles I - King of England, Scotland and Ireland

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/charles-i
Reign: 1625 - 1649
Born-Died: 1600 - 1649
Dynasty: House of Stuart
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_I_of_England

Charles I's disputes with Parliament over taxation, religion and royal authority culminated in the Wars of the Three Kingdoms. Defeat led to his trial and execution for high treason in 1649, after which monarchy was abolished.
