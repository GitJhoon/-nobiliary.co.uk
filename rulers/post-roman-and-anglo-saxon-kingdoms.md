# Post-Roman and Anglo-Saxon Kingdoms - Multiple British, Anglo-Saxon and Gaelic rulers

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/post-roman-and-anglo-saxon-kingdoms
Reign: c. 410 - 802
Dynasty: Early Kingdoms
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heptarchy

After Roman rule ended, Britain fragmented into many competing kingdoms. Brittonic rulers, Anglo-Saxon dynasties, Picts, Scots and later Viking forces controlled different regions, so there was no single monarch of Britain or even of England.

## Succession
- Preceded by: [Roman Britain](https://english-history.com/rulers/roman-britain)
- Succeeded by: [Egbert](https://english-history.com/rulers/egbert)
