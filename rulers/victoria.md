# Victoria - Queen of the United Kingdom; Empress of India

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/victoria
Reign: 1837 - 1901
Born-Died: 1819 - 1901
Dynasty: House of Hanover
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Victoria

Victoria's sixty-three-year reign coincided with industrialization, urbanization, imperial expansion and the emergence of modern mass politics. Although formal royal power diminished, the monarchy acquired a powerful new public and family symbolism.

## Succession
- Preceded by: [William IV](https://english-history.com/rulers/william-iv)
- Succeeded by: [Edward VII](https://english-history.com/rulers/edward-vii)
