# Henry V - King of England

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/henry-v
Reign: 1413 - 1422
Born-Died: 1386 - 1422
Dynasty: House of Lancaster
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_V_of_England

Henry V revived the English claim to France and achieved a spectacular victory at Agincourt. The Treaty of Troyes made him heir to the French throne, but his early death left an infant son to inherit two kingdoms at war.
