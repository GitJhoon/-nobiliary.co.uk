# Henry III - King of England

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/henry-iii
Reign: 1216 - 1272
Born-Died: 1207 - 1272
Dynasty: Plantagenets
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_III_of_England

Henry III became king as a child during civil war and ruled for more than five decades. His costly policies and disputes with the barons culminated in the Provisions of Oxford and Simon de Montfort's rebellion, important stages in Parliament's development.

- Preceded by: [John](https://english-history.com/rulers/john)
