# Oliver Cromwell - Lord Protector of England, Scotland and Ireland

URL: https://english-history.com/rulers/oliver-cromwell
Reign: 1653 - 1658
Born-Died: 1599 - 1658
Dynasty: Protectorate
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oliver_Cromwell

Oliver Cromwell became Lord Protector under the written constitution known as the Instrument of Government. His regime combined civilian institutions with strong military power and extended a single Protectorate administration across England, Scotland and Ireland.
