About the project

History is never finished

History of Britain is an independent digital history project tracing Britain’s story from its earliest human presence to the modern United Kingdom through timelines, rulers, turning points and visual storytelling.

The project brings political, social, military and cultural history into one exploratory experience. It connects individual events to the wider forces that shaped England, Scotland, Wales, Ireland, the British Empire and Britain’s changing place in the world.

History is never a single, finished story. The site aims to present complex and contested subjects clearly while directing readers toward sources for deeper study.

Author

Historical research, content curation, and interface design by Robertas Borysevicius.

This project was conceived and built as a personal passion project dedicated to making British history engaging, accessible and easy to explore across centuries.
